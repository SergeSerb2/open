Three Days in Lisbon

Tiled stairways, a river that behaves like a sea, and the best custard tarts you will eat this year.

By Ana Ferro · 8 min read

Start high. The viewpoint above Alfama opens at dawn, and the light comes off the river in long gold strokes. Walk down through the lanes before the trams fill up.

By midday, cross to the riverside market for grilled sardines and a cold glass of white. In the afternoon, the tile museum is quiet and cool.

Day oneAlfama, the castle, a fado bar.
Day twoBelém, pastries, the river walk.
Day threeSintra by train, palaces in fog.
