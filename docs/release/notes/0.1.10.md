# Open 0.1.10 (12)

Engine: Chromium 156.0.8078.48 · CEF 4fa4ab5. macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon.

This release fixes Microsoft Outlook sign-in and cross-site authentication/form flows opening in Peek, and fixes Easel shapes not redrawing when resized.

## Sign-in, passkeys, and Peek
- Fixed Microsoft Outlook sign-in failing with `AADSTS10040141: The request is invalid.` when signing in from a Pinned tab, Favorite, or Peek.
- Prevented `POST` requests, form submissions (`POST` and `GET`), client/server redirects, and history traversals from being cancelled and reopened as `GET` in Peek or Little Open.
- Exempted cross-site identity, SSO, OAuth, SAML, and FIDO/passkey bridge flows from automatic Peek on Pinned and Favorite tabs while preserving explicit Shift-click Peek and ordinary external link Peek.

## Easels
- Fixed Easel shapes and canvas objects not redrawing when resized until another property change forced a repaint.
