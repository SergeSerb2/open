# Open 0.1.5 (7)

Engine: Chromium 156.0.8078.48 · CEF 4fa4ab5. macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon.

## Pinned tabs and Favorites
- The × on a pinned tab closes it, also after you reopen Open.
- On the selected pinned tab, − unloads the tab and keeps it in the sidebar.
- ⌘W on a pinned tab or a Favorite takes it back to its pinned page. Open then selects the tab you used before it.
- Favorites no longer show a close button.
- After a tab closes, the row that moves under the pointer shows its hover state at once.

## Motion
- Split View: panes move smoothly when you add, remove or close one. Close This Pane keeps the page visible while its pane closes.
- Switching Spaces: the sidebar starts to slide as soon as you click, press a key or swipe, and it no longer pauses at the end.
- Peek shows its card at once and fades in the page when the page is ready.

## Other changes
- An app icon that you choose in Settings now has the same size as other icons in the Dock.
- The Find bar fits in narrow Split View panes.
- Fixes for bugs found in reviews of settings, the command bar, saved windows and tabs, the browser window, the Library and media.
