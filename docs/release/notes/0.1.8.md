# Open 0.1.8 (10)

Engine: Chromium 156.0.8078.48 · CEF 4fa4ab5. macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon.

This release is about how Open looks and feels. We reviewed every screen pixel by pixel and fixed more than 100 visual and interaction problems.

## Sidebar and Spaces
- The Space switcher no longer runs out of the sidebar when you have many Spaces. It scrolls, fades at the edges and keeps the current Space in view.
- The Space list no longer goes blank after you switch Spaces. Letter and number Space icons are no longer black and heavy.
- The New Space form shows again from the menu, the "+" button and the command bar. Create Space stays off until the Space has a name.
- Long tab titles redraw after you resize the sidebar. Closing the selected Today tab no longer flashes an empty row.
- Switching into an empty or new Space no longer pops its background in late.

## Onboarding and import
- The name field, buttons and footers no longer clip or sit on the edge.
- The import report shows passwords, cookies, addresses and cards as clear rows, and says "None found" instead of "0 imported".
- Checkboxes use your accent color. The "Skip" labels agree.

## Library, Notes and Easels
- The Library rail no longer cuts the last tile in short windows. Media previews show real thumbnails.
- Library > Spaces scrolls sideways with a trackpad or mouse wheel.
- Escape leaves Easel text editing cleanly. Note images no longer have a blank band.

## Command bar, Find and Little Open
- Hovering a link shows its address again.
- Little Open is readable in Light mode. Find text no longer clips.
- Tabs with the same title show their Space and site, so you can tell them apart.
- The selected row has enough contrast on every Space color.

## Settings, prompts and windows
- Settings uses one style for pop-up menus, buttons and sheets. Keyboard focus is visible.
- The permission prompt stays inside the window and is opaque in Light mode. Update status shows in the Updates card.
- New windows open next to the window you came from, with its size and sidebar width. Window titles show the Space name.
- Split View pane bars hide buttons in narrow panes instead of overlapping them.

## Accessibility
- VoiceOver can reach the sidebar tabs. Unloaded tabs have a label.
- Increase Contrast now also lifts site names, the selected row and toolbar icons.
