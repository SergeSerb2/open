# Open 0.1.0 (1)

Engine: Chromium 156.0.8078.48 · CEF 4fa4ab5. macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon.

The first release of Open: a Mac browser that keeps your tabs in a sidebar, organised by Spaces.

## Sidebar and tabs
- Favorites, Pinned tabs, folders and Today tabs in one sidebar. Drag a tab to pin it, unpin it, reorder it or move it into a folder.
- Today tabs archive themselves after the time you choose. Find them again in the Library.
- Show or hide the sidebar with ⌘S. Hover the window edge to open it for a moment.
- Hover previews for folders and pinned tabs, and live cards for calendar and mail sites.
- Live Folders keep a folder up to date with your items from GitHub.

## Spaces and Profiles
- Spaces with their own colours and themes. Switch with a swipe, a click or a keyboard shortcut.
- Profiles keep their own history, cookies, passwords and extensions. Each Space can use a different Profile.

## Command bar and windows
- The command bar (⌘T, ⌘L) searches the web, your tabs, history and Open's commands.
- Split View, Peek for links that open in a pop-over, and Little Open for links from other apps.
- Link routing rules send links from other apps to the Space you choose.
- Join a meeting from your calendar with ⌘⌃J.

## Library, Notes and Easels
- The Library shows Downloads, Archive, History, Media, Spaces, Boosts and Easels, and can include media from your Documents, Desktop, Screenshots and Downloads folders.
- Notes: rich-text notes in the sidebar, with Markdown shortcuts, images and links. Export a note as a file.
- Easels: canvases for pictures, text, shapes, web clips and live web cards.

## Sites
- Built-in ad and tracker blocking, on from the first page.
- Site Control: permissions, site data and ad blocking for the site you are on.
- Boosts change how a site looks or behaves; Zap removes parts of a page.
- Chrome Web Store extensions, with their toolbar buttons and pop-ups.
- Password manager: save, fill and manage passwords, cards and addresses.
- DRM-protected video (Widevine) can be turned on in Settings ▸ Advanced ▸ Play DRM-protected video. It is off by default and takes effect after you reopen Open.

## Move in
- Import from Arc: Spaces, tabs, folders, bookmarks, history, Boosts, Notes, Easels, passwords, cookies, cards and addresses.
- Import from Chrome, Brave, Edge and other Chromium browsers, Firefox and Safari: bookmarks and history, open tabs from Chromium browsers and Firefox, and passwords (with your approval). Cookies, cards and addresses come from Chromium browsers.
- A first-run guide sets up your Favorites, theme and ad blocking.

## Updates
- Open checks for updates automatically (Settings ▸ General ▸ Update Open automatically), or when you choose Check for Updates. It downloads the update and installs it when you choose Install and Relaunch.

## Known issues
- iCloud Keychain passkeys are not available. Security keys and phone passkeys work.
- Firefox form data and Safari open tabs are not imported.
